# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to uphold it.

In short: be kind, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and leave harassment and personal attacks out entirely.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@tinker20](https://github.com/tinker20), privately. Every report will be reviewed, and the reporter's privacy will be respected. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors who repeatedly or seriously break it.
